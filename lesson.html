塔万达
王主管，请过来看一下！

Hǎo de. Yǒu wèntí ma?

This machine has a problem.
王主管
我检查一下。……这台设备有故障。请关机。
Wǒ jiǎnchá yíxià. …… Zhè tái shèbèi yǒu gùzhàng. Qǐng guānjī.
Let me check. ... This machine has a fault. Please shut it down.
塔万达
好的。谢谢！
Hǎo de. Xièxie!
OK. Thank you!
3

Grammar points语言点

1. 这 + 量词 + 名词 this + measure word + noun

这台设备有故障。
Zhè tái shèbèi yǒu gùzhàng.
This machine has a fault. (机器用"台")

2. V + 一下 do it briefly (softens a request)

请过来看一下。
Qǐng guòlai kàn yíxià.
Please come and have a look (briefly).
